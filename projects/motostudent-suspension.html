MotoStudent Suspension Design

Excel · MATLAB · SolidWorks | Sept 2021 – Oct 2023

Project Context

Competition: MotoStudent Competition VII Edition Team: MotoStudent Unizar Racing Team Timeframe: Sept 2021 – Oct 2023 Tools: Excel (vehicle dynamics), MATLAB (kinematics automation), SolidWorks (CAD) Objective: Design progressive rear suspension system for two prototype motorcycles

Vehicle Dynamics Analysis

📊 Excel Rigid Bike Model

Spreadsheet-based model exploring weight distribution, wheelbase/rake/trail geometry, grip ellipse behavior, and stability limits (wheelie/loss of grip scenarios)

🔧 Component Load Calculations

Force calculations for all chassis components → essential input for structural design, FEA validation, material selection, and mounting point reinforcement

Progressive Suspension Design

🎯 Design Target

50% motion ratio increase over suspension travel → progressive spring characteristics. Provides excellent small bump sensitivity at beginning of travel while preventing bottoming on large impacts. Improves traction management during acceleration/braking

Kinematic Analysis Evolution

📐 Initial Phase: Tony Foale Software

Established baseline linkage configurations and identified feasible design directions

⚡ MATLAB Automation

Custom script for rapid design iteration: automated kinematic calculations, target-based optimization (progressiveness + force minimization), batch analysis. Dramatically reduced design cycle time → explored wider design space

Mechanical Design

🔧 SolidWorks CAD Design

Detailed assembly design balancing performance with manufacturing feasibility and competition budget constraints
